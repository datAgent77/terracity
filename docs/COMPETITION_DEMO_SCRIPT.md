# TerraCity — competition demo script (5 minutes)

**One city. Two futures. Your decisions.**

Setup: Quest 3 / 3S, Meta Quest Browser, seated, hands (controllers as
fallback). Open the live build, tap **Enter Mixed Reality**. Sound on.
Desktop fallback: the same flow with mouse and keyboard (keys in brackets).

Every number and sentence shown during the demo comes from the simulation
of the plan being played; nothing below is scripted output.

| Time | Beat | What the user does | What answers (from the model) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:45 | **Arrival** | Sees the miniature coastal town; card "Every city begins with a choice." → **Begin** | The coast, the town, farmland, ridge |
| 0:45–1:45 | **Decision** | Step 1: pinch **Homes** from the tray, drop it on a glowing tile beside the town [1, click] | Card "What your decision changes — Now (2026): Housed +4,000 …; By 2050: Newcomers in unplanned sprawl −4,000 … Region-wide." |
| 1:45–2:30 | **Time** | Step 2: turn the time dial (or tap **2050**) [T] | The city in 2050; the card re-evaluates the decision *by 2050*; pinch the card to step through causes [N] |
| 2:30–3:15 | **Split Reality** | Step 3: hands together, pull apart (or **Split**) [V] | Your plan vs the same coast if nobody plans, side by side, with a comparison strip |
| 3:15–3:45 | **Reality Seam** | Step 4: **Reality Seam**, pinch the golden handle and sweep [G, ← →] | Both futures on the table at the same place; under the seam: "A Your plan: homes … / B If nobody plans: unplanned houses …" |
| 3:45–4:40 | **Meet Mira** | Step 5: **Enter future**, then pinch Mira / **Talk to Mira** [E, K] | Lines grounded in that future (e.g. "The trees in our park have grown so much." — she points at the park); Step 6: return, enter the other future: the same place, other words |
| 4:40–5:00 | **Change it** | Back at the table | "The future isn't written. Change it." — **Keep planning** / **Replay intro** |

Talking points (one sentence each):

- *Decision*: "It houses 4,000 people now; by 2050 it also keeps 4,000
  newcomers out of unplanned sprawl — a region-wide effect, shared with your
  other homes."
- *Seam*: "Same tile, same year, two plans — what you see on each side is
  that plan's own projection."
- *Mira*: "She only says what the simulation supports where she stands."

If something goes wrong: **Skip tips** / **Replay intro** (Plan pad → More)
restarts the journey without losing the plan; **Return to tabletop** is
always on the street panel.
