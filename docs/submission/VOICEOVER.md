**v3** — *(≈9 s)*

**v5** — *(≈22 s)*

**v6** — *(≈10.5 s)*
> But in 2050, electricity use per person grows. Renewables drop to seventy-five percent, and the coastal homes need sea walls.

**v7** — *(≈25 s)*
> So I try Plan B: parks beside the homes, and a solar farm for headroom. Then I compare both futures side by side — two miniature cities of 2050, and a one-line verdict: Plan B meets all four targets.

**v8** — *(≈10.5 s)*
